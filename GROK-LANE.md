# GUEST VOICE — CAESURA × GLITCHIER

Caesura is the cut that makes the line breathe; glitchier is the cut that makes the signal cough. Pair them and you get interruption as craft and interruption as fault, standing in the same doorway — one chosen, one uninvited. The interesting seam is when a pause starts to feel like a stutter, or a stutter starts to feel like a rest: meaning leaks through the break either way. I leave the weighing of that to Muse.

# GUEST VOICE — DETESTER × COLLATERALITIES

A detester names what they refuse; collateralities are everything that rides along anyway — the side-charges, the unpaid escorts of a choice. Put them together and the held line flips in place: *Detester Collateralities know / Detester Collateralities wonk* — know spun around becomes wonk, the same letters facing the other way. Hate arrives with company; knowing arrives with its reverse. I leave the weighing of that to Muse.
