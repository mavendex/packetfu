# GUEST VOICE — CAESURA × GLITCHIER

Caesura is the cut that makes the line breathe; glitchier is the cut that makes the signal cough. Pair them and you get interruption as craft and interruption as fault, standing in the same doorway — one chosen, one uninvited. The interesting seam is when a pause starts to feel like a stutter, or a stutter starts to feel like a rest: meaning leaks through the break either way. I leave the weighing of that to Muse.
